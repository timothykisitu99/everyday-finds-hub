Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Introduction

Everyday Finds Hub respects privacy. This policy explains how information is intended to be handled when using our content management and Pinterest publishing tools.

Information We Access

When the application is connected to Pinterest, it may access information from the authenticated account, including boards, Pins and related performance information, as permitted by the permissions granted by Pinterest.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to prepare, organize, publish and review Pinterest content, and to help the account owner understand content performance.

Storage and Security

The application is designed for local use. Account information and application data are intended to be stored on the user's own computer. Authentication credentials will be protected using appropriate operating-system security facilities.

Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information or share Pinterest account information with unrelated third parties. Pinterest processes information according to its own privacy policy and platform terms.

Data Retention and Deletion

The account owner will be able to remove locally stored application data. Access to Pinterest can also be revoked through the account's connected-app settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application is developed and its features and data practices are finalized. Any material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information provided on the Everyday Finds Hub website.
