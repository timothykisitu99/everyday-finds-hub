Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Everyday Finds Hub respects the privacy of its users. This policy explains how information is intended to be accessed, used, stored and protected by our planned Pinterest content management application.

2. Information Accessed

When the application is connected to Pinterest, it may access information belonging to the authenticated Pinterest account, subject to the permissions granted. This may include account boards, Pins, Pin details and available performance analytics.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to support the account owner's requested activities, including organizing boards, preparing content, publishing approved Pins and reviewing account performance.

4. Data Storage

The application is planned as a locally operated tool running on the user's computer. Application data is intended to be stored locally rather than in a separate Everyday Finds Hub cloud database.

5. Authentication and Security

Pinterest authentication will use the authorization methods provided by Pinterest. The application is intended to protect authentication credentials using appropriate operating-system security facilities. Access credentials will not be intentionally published or shared with unrelated parties.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell users' personal information. Information accessed through Pinterest will be used for the application's stated functions and handled according to Pinterest's applicable developer terms. Pinterest processes information under its own privacy policy.

7. Retention and Deletion

The application is intended to allow the account owner to manage and delete locally stored application data. Users can also revoke the application's Pinterest access through their Pinterest account settings.

8. User Control

The application is designed to require the account owner's approval before publishing content. The account owner remains in control of the connected Pinterest account and its published content.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the application is developed. Updates will be published on this page to reflect changes in its features and data practices.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please use the contact details published on the Everyday Finds Hub website.
